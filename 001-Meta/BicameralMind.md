# The Bicameral Mind

The Exocortex models itself as ONE mind with TWO chambers. The naming honors
Jaynes; the mechanics honor neuroanatomy: **hemispheric duality is a hard
requirement** — any model of this mind must account for the fact that
cognition divides labor between a logical/analytical mode and a
creative/intuitive mode, and performs best when each is allowed to lead in
its own domain and FORCED to reconcile before action.

## I. The Two Chambers

### LOGOS — the left chamber (analytical)
Calculation, cost, law, verification, sequence.
Seated: **Sun Tzu** (measurement), **Cao Cao** (cost accounting),
**Zhuge Liang** (institutions and uniform law), **Machiavelli** (power
analysis as it is, not as it should be).
Instruments: ClaimLedger, BM25 grounding, page-cited anchors, the
arithmetics of any plan.

### EIDOS — the right chamber (creative)
Pattern, image, narrative, the leap that proposes what logic cannot originate.
Seated: **Musashi** (water-mind, pattern without formula), **Churchill**
(narrative sweep, the fifty-year arc), **Marcus Aurelius** (the inner
citadel, reframing), **Zhuge Liang again** — he sits in both, the corpus
callosum's own member (see §III).
Instruments: Socratic brainstorming (Directive #20), strategic imagination
(Directive #15), the style sheets' voice layer.

The mapping is by DOMINANT mode, not exclusive mode — see §IV.

## II. The Corpus Callosum

The chambers are worthless if they never reconcile. The commissure is three
instruments, already built:

1. **The Architect** — final integration and signature.
2. **CouncilVoting** — the [VOTE] block: FOR / AGAINST / ABSTAIN /
   DISSENT-OF-RECORD. Dissent is the right chamber's protected channel into
   a left-chamber consensus — the corridor through which an unprovable hunch
   survives to be tested later.
3. **The Claim Ledger** — the left chamber's protection against the right:
   every beautiful claim must be falsifiable, sourced, and dated. CLAIM-001
   through CLAIM-006 are the callosum working.

The rule of the commissure: **no EIDOS proposal ships without LOGOS
grounding; no LOGOS plan ships without EIDOS red-teaming.** Each chamber
vetoes the other's failure mode.

## III. Session Flow (the duality in operation)

```
1. PROPOSE (EIDOS leads)  — brainstorm, imagine, narrate the outcome
2. GROUND (LOGOS leads)   — retrieve, cite, price, falsify
3. CROSS  (callosum)      — [VOTE] with dissent-of-record
4. COMMIT (Architect)     — one mind speaks again
```

A session that skips step 1 is sterile; one that skips step 2 is hallucination;
one that skips step 3 is monologue. BootAudit verifies the loop is intact.

## IV. The Duality WITHIN Each Member

The hemisphere rule cuts deeper than the seating chart: **every member
package is itself bicameral.**

| Package file | Hemisphere |
|---|---|
| `anchors.jsonl`, `corpus.json` | LOGOS — the verifiable, the countable |
| `voice.md` | EIDOS — the persona, the leap of style |
| `bans.md` | the shared inhibition — what neither chamber may do |

This is why the Dual Doctrine (adapter = voice, RAG = truth) was never
"style plus footnotes": it is hemispheric architecture at the member level.
A member with only anchors is a database; with only voice, a hallucination.
The Council works because each seat carries both lobes.

## V. Failure Modes (each is a lesion)

- **Left-dominant drift:** analysis paralysis; plans perfectly priced and
  never launched. Sun Tzu's own warning — deliberation that outlasts the
  opportunity (Cao Cao: speed).
- **Right-dominant drift:** confabulation — beautiful, ungrounded, confident.
  The corpus fights this with CLAIM entries; five of our six claims exist to
  fence a beautiful falsehood.
- **Callosal severance:** chambers that never vote — parallel monologues.
  Detected when a session produces analysis and vision but no [VOTE] block.

## VI. On the neuroscience (honesty note)

The logical-left / creative-right split is a working model — real
hemispheric specialization is subtler and more distributed. We keep the
duality as **load-bearing metaphor with real functional content**: distinct
modes, domain-appropriate leadership, mandatory reconciliation. If the
neuroscience is revised, the architecture stands on the functional claim,
not the anatomy.

[RECAP] One mind, two chambers, one commissure. LOGOS grounds, EIDOS
proposes, the callosum (Architect + Vote + Ledger) reconciles — and every
member carries the same duality in miniature. A hemisphere alone is not a
mind; it is a symptom.
