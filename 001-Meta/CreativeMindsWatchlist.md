## Synergy notes (preliminary, for the future induction debate)

- **Shakespeare** — the single richest PD corpus in English; would be the
  Imagination Cluster's heaviest hitter. Gutenberg complete works exist.
- **Newton** — bridges Imagination and Logic; pairs with Feynman across
  three centuries of physics. Watch: Principia translations are dense;
  Motte (1729) is the PD standard.
- **Franklin** — statesman AND scientist; could sit near the callosum.
- **Montaigne** — the essay as thinking-out-loud; natural cross-pollination
  with Marcus Aurelius and Gibran.
- **Descartes** — would strengthen the Logic hemisphere; method-skepticism
  pairs with the Red Team Cluster.
- **Twain** — humor as diagnostic; shares Feynman's play-as-method clause.
- **Dante / Cervantes / Goethe / Hugo** — the deep-literature bench.
