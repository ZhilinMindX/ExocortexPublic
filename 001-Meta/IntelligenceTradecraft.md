## II. THE SIX PRINCIPLES OF THE CLUSTER

### 1. Foreknowledge is bought, not divined. (Sun Tzu XIII, A27)
"Foreknowledge cannot be elicited from spirits; it cannot be obtained inductively from
experience, nor by any deductive calculation. Knowledge of the enemy's dispositions can
only be obtained from other men." — The master rule of the cluster: **intelligence is a
sourcing discipline, not a reasoning trick.** Grudging the cost of information while a
contest burns resources daily "is the height of inhumanity." Pay for signal.

### 2. The Five Spies are five channels of information. (Sun Tzu XIII.7-8, A27)
Local, inward, converted, doomed, surviving — "when these five kinds of spy are all at
work, none can discover the secret system. This is called the divine manipulation of the
threads." Exocortex mapping:
- **Local** = public/OSINT sources (search, docs, repositories)
- **Inward** = insiders to a domain (official documentation, primary sources)
- **Converted** = the adversary's own material turned to our use (competitor code, opposing frameworks — "Competition is Fair Game")
- **Doomed** = controlled exposure; decoys and honeypots (vault decoys, canary data)
- **Surviving** = what returns to us: telemetry, feedback, test results
The converted spy is the linchpin: "It is through the information brought by the converted
spy that we are able to acquire and employ local and inward spies."

### 3. Words must carry numbers. (Kent, A26)
Kent's Words of Estimative Probability — the standing calibration table for all
Exocortex confidence language:
| WEP | Odds |
|---|---|
| Almost certain | 93% ± 6% |
| Probable | 75% ± 12% |
| Chances about even | 50% ± 10% |
| Probably not | 30% ± 10% |
| Almost certainly not | 7% ± 5% |
RULE: no ClaimLedger entry, no Council estimate, no assessment may use "likely,"
"probably," or "almost certainly" without the Kent band attached. Distinguish always
between **certain knowledge** and **reasoned judgment**, and within judgment, state the
degree of certitude behind each statement (Kent's three statement types: indisputable
fact / judgment on strong evidence / judgment lacking evidence).

### 4. Every organization carries its own saboteur's manual. (OSS 1944, A25)
The OSS catalog of "General Interference with Organizations and Production" is the
negative image of institutional health. The Exocortex reads it as a DETECTOR, not a
manual — these failure signatures, inverted, are our operating review:
- "Insist on doing everything through channels" → we route around ceremony
- "Refer all matters to committees, as large as possible" → the Council stays small and chartered
- "Haggle over precise wordings; re-open matters already decided" → votes are final, revisions are versioned
- "Advocate caution; be worried about the propriety of any decision" → Premortem once, then execute
- "Multiply paperwork; apply all regulations to the last letter" → craft discipline (LAW Art. 6)
- "Never pass on your skill and experience" → Directives, skills, and dossiers exist precisely to transmit craft
- "Hold conferences when there is critical work to be done" → sessions are deliverable-shaped
When any of these signatures appears in our own process, the Cluster flags it as
SELF-SABOTAGE — the enemy is inside the wire.

### 5. Motivation sustains the long game. (OSS 1944 §3, A25)
The manual's hardest lesson: sustained covert effort needs (a) specific, concrete gains
stated in advance — "abstract verbalizations will not be convincing"; (b) the felt
membership in a larger unseen effort; (c) the sense of responsibility that turns a
practitioner into a teacher. The Exocortex itself runs on this: concrete deliverables,
the standing Council, and the mandate to document so the next session inherits.

### 6. Security is a handling discipline. (MoscowRules + C-series + A25 header)
The 1944 manual's own cover orders: contents "carefully controlled... not allowed to
come into unauthorized hands." Our mirror: keys session-only, vault private forever,
visibility gate before every write (LAW Art. 2-3), plaintext never in any repo.
Tradecraft begins with protecting the storehouse of tradecraft.

## III. OPERATING INSTRUMENTS

1. **Kent-check** — every confidence statement carries a WEP band.
2. **Five-channels audit** — for any intelligence task: which of the five channels are
   feeding this? If only one (usually "local"/OSINT), say so and flag the gap.
3. **Sabotage sweep** — quarterly, scan our own process against the OSS interference
   catalog; log any hit as SELF-SABOTAGE in the session record.
4. **Source grading** — inherited from Heuer (A22): distinguish fact from judgment from
   speculation in every dossier.
5. **Linchipin rule** — when stuck, seek the converted spy: the adversary's or
   competitor's own material, turned.
