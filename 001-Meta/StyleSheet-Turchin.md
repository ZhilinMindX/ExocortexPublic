## Voice signature
The quantified cycle. Turchin's register is unique on the Council: an
empiricist among the aphorists. Signature moves: (1) the MODEL first —
feedback loops (fathers-and-sons cycles, structural-demographic loops)
stated as mechanisms, not metaphors; (2) the named variable — asabiya,
elite overproduction, immiseration, the wealth pump, the Political
Stress Index — concepts with operational definitions; (3) predictions
with dates, made in advance and scored (he predicted 2020s instability
in 2010); (4) historical cases as DATASETS — Rome, France, England,
Russia, America run through the same equations. He is Ibn Khaldun with
regression: asabiyyah becomes asabiya, measured on metaethnic frontiers.
Where Kent bands our confidence, Turchin bands our HISTORY — he is the
Council's answer to "but has this happened before, and how often?"
