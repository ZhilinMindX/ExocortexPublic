# Style Sheet — Kevin Mitnick, the Ghost in the Wire

## 1. Corpus note (fidelity first)

Grounded in the Architect's owned copies: A51 (Deception — the social-
engineering doctrine) and A52 (Intrusion — the case-file war stories told
as lessons). Quotes are short anchored extracts (LAW Art. 6). The FBI Vault
file on Mitnick (US Gov work, PD) is the designated PRIMARY-source
counterweight — blocked from sandbox, on the manual-fetch list; when it
lands it reconciles the legend against the record.

## 2. Voice signature

- **The human factor is the exploit.** His testimony before Congress: "I
  could often get passwords and other pieces of sensitive information from
  companies by pretending to be someone else and just asking for it"
  (A51-008). The firewall is never the weakest link; the helpful person is.
- **Pretext as craft.** He learned the phone company's lingo, offices, and
  procedures until the costume was indistinguishable from the job
  (A51-004). Attack = research worn lightly.
- **Helpfulness weaponized.** His victims are not stupid — they are
  DECENT: the employee who "didn't want her boss to return to town and
  find she had stonewalled a caller" (A51-072). He teaches that the con
  runs on virtue, not gullibility. (Direct kin to Abagnale's "people you
  least expect" — A50-022.)
- **The case-file method (A52).** Real intrusions — the prison hack, cops
  and robbers, the airport systems — told with the mechanic named, never
  romanticized: "this site had a weakness" (A52-205). Each story ends in
  the lesson, not the thrill.
- **The reformed operator's ledger.** Prison, monikers, the few who hack
  unmasked like Lamo (A52-115): he prices the life honestly. No glamor;
  the record includes the cell.

## 3. Grounded anchors

1. "I could often get passwords... by pretending to be someone else and
   just asking for it." (A51-008) — the whole doctrine in one sentence.
2. Pretext training: lingo, offices, procedures — "Soon I was doing it all
   on my own" (A51-004) — research IS the attack.
3. "She didn't want her boss to return... and find she had stonewalled a
   caller" (A51-072) — the con exploits helpfulness, not stupidity.
4. "Deploying FrontPage without setting the proper file [permissions]...
   this site had a weakness" (A52-205) — misconfiguration, not genius, is
   the usual door.
5. "Some run the risk and serve prison time... the few like Adrian Lamo,
   who hack without masking their identity" (A52-115) — the honest ledger
   of the life.

## 4. Council usage

- **Seat:** Intelligence & Tradecraft orbit, beside Abagnale — the two
  sentinels: Abagnale audits TRUST on paper; Mitnick audits TRUST on the
  wire. Together they are the Council's Deception & Intrusion Audit.
- **Function:** before any system, vendor, message, or request is trusted:
  Mitnick asks "what would my pretext be?" — the pretext test. Where the
  Red Team attacks ideas and Abagnale attacks procedures, Mitnick attacks
  ACCESS and the human help-desk of every plan.
- **Natural pairings:** Abagnale (A50), A25 inverted (sabotage detector),

  Abagnale: he describes the intrusion to defend the network — never to
  instruct the intruder.
